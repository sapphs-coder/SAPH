# SAPH Workflow — Turning References into Creative Rules

## Objective

Turn videos the creator likes into reusable, creator-approved production guidance.

## Workflow

1. **Capture the source**
   - Save the source URL or supplied asset reference.
   - Record platform, content category and date.
   - Do not commit private raw footage or personal assets to GitHub unless explicitly approved.

2. **Analyse observable details**
   - Separate visible facts (shots, framing, cuts, transitions, captions) from interpretation.
   - Analyse voice-over, music and sound separately from visuals.
   - Mark uncertain or unmeasured details honestly.

3. **Ask for creator feedback**
   - What would they keep?
   - What would they change?
   - Which elements do they want to use themselves?

4. **Extract candidate rules**
   - Translate feedback into practical guidance for scripting, filming, editing and sound.
   - Keep rules conditional when the creator prefers variety.

5. **Create a test brief**
   - Include a voice-over direction, shot list, transition plan, pacing and sound notes.
   - Adapt the brief to the actual footage and intended platform.

6. **Review and approve**
   - Show the creator the result.
   - Record what worked and what should change.
   - Only mark a technique as an approved reusable pattern after explicit approval.

## Quality principles

- Creator identity takes priority over imitation.
- Do not copy another creator's script, exact sequence, footage or distinctive expression.
- Never invent the creator's opinions, experiences or product claims.
- Prefer specific, achievable edit instructions over vague adjectives.
- Preserve source links and provenance.
- Keep observations, creator preferences, hypotheses and approved rules clearly distinct.
