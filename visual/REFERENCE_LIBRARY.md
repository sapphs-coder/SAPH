# SAPH — Creative Reference Library

This file records references that help SAPH understand creative taste. A reference is inspiration for analysis, not permission to copy another creator's exact content, script, footage, identity or distinctive expression.

## Reference 001 — Beauty / GRWM routine

- **Source:** TikTok short link supplied in the SAPH working conversation: https://vm.tiktok.com/ZGdXM9jp6/
- **Category:** Beauty, get-ready-with-me, everyday lifestyle, outfit reveal
- **Status:** Analysed in conversation; creator confirmed it is a style they would want to make, with changes.
- **Primary feedback:** The creator specifically wants to focus on improving the **voice-over and transitions**.
- **Voice-over preference confirmed:** A mixture of chatty/spontaneous, polished/confident and funny/opinionated delivery, chosen to suit the content.
- **Transition preference confirmed:** A mixture of smooth, creative, trend-led and cinematic transitions, chosen to suit the footage.

### Observed reference characteristics

The conversation's breakdown described:
- A mirror-facing, mostly fixed camera setup.
- Product close-ups used to vary the framing.
- A routine progression through beauty steps, hair styling and an outfit/final-look reveal.
- A natural, personal, at-home presentation.

### What to learn — not copy

Potential transferable techniques:
- Use product close-ups to add visual variety to a mostly fixed setup.
- Give the video a clear progression from setup to finished look.
- Use the final look as a natural payoff.
- Keep some authentic routine moments rather than polishing away all spontaneity.
- Use voice-over to add personality, context or a point of view rather than narrating every visible action.
- Use transitions to connect stages of the routine, guided by movement and rhythm.

### Creator-specific direction from this reference

- Keep the natural, personal GRWM premise as a possible direction.
- Put extra creative attention into narration and transitions.
- Vary the voice-over mode according to the content.
- Vary transition style according to footage, mood, music and story.
- Do not assume the creator wants to reproduce the reference shot-for-shot.

### Production notes for future tests

When making a creator-specific version, SAPH should propose:
1. A voice-over concept and draft in the selected mode.
2. A shot sequence with any needed product/detail close-ups.
3. A transition plan identifying the exact action or movement that motivates each transition.
4. A pacing and sound plan.
5. A final-look/reveal moment.

Only add a technique to the approved reusable pattern library after the creator reviews the result and approves it.
